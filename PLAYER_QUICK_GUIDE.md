## 🗑️ NEW FEATURES: Trash Can Hints & Teleportation

### Understanding Trash Cans

When you look at a trash can, you'll see:

1. **Bin Type** - What category (Recyclable, Biodegradable, etc.)
2. **Description** - What the bin is for
3. **💡 HINT** - A helpful tip WITHOUT spoiling the answer
4. **Examples** - Common items in that category

**Example:**

```
RECYCLABLE BIN

For materials that belong in the recycling stream.

💡 HINT: This bin accepts materials that can be 
processed and reused. Look for items made of 
paper, plastic, or metal.

Examples: Cardboard, glass bottles, aluminum cans,
plastic bottles
```

### Teleportation Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **T** | Teleport to NEAREST trash can |
| **1** | Teleport to Trash Can #1 |
| **2** | Teleport to Trash Can #2 |
| **3** | Teleport to Trash Can #3 |
| **4** | Trash Can #4 |
| **5** | Trash Can #5 |

**How to Use:**

- Holding waste and want to reach another bin quickly? Press **T**
- Want to check out a specific bin? Press **1-5**
- Will instantly teleport you there!

## 📚 Hints System Explained

The hints are designed to HELP YOU THINK, not give away answers:

### Recyclable Bin

- 💡 "Look for items made of paper, plastic, or metal"
- ✓ Teaches you to recognize materials
- ✗ NOT: "Put soda cans here" (too direct!)

### Biodegradable Bin

- 💡 "Think about materials from nature that decompose naturally"
- ✓ Teaches you about organic waste
- ✗ NOT: "This is for food" (spoiler!)

### Hazardous Bin

- 💡 "Look for items that require special handling"
- ✓ Teaches you about danger/caution
- ✗ NOT: "This is for batteries" (answer!)

## 🎯 Playing Strategy

### Step 1: Learn the Categories

Read hints for each trash can type to understand:

- What materials they accept
- Why they're important
- How waste is classified

### Step 2: Observe and Learn

- Pick up waste items
- Read their descriptions
- Use hints to make your best guess
- See if you're correct!

### Step 3: Build Your Knowledge

- After 3-4 correct disposals, patterns emerge
- You'll recognize items by sight
- Disposal becomes faster and easier

### Step 4: Challenge Yourself

- Try to correctly sort without checking hints
- See if you've truly learned the categories
- Compete with friends for high scores

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Read Everything** - Item descriptions and hints contain clues
2. **Look at the Examples** - Real-world items help you understand
3. **Wrong? That's Learning!** - Mistakes teach you where items actually belong
4. **Use Teleportation** - Don't waste time running; use T and number keys
5. **Check Help** - Press H anytime to review controls

## 🏆 Success Tips

- **Slow down** - Read hints carefully
- **Think logically** - What materials are recyclable?
- **Ask yourself** - "Does this break down naturally?"
- **Remember examples** - They're real-world items
- **Learn by doing** - Don't be afraid to try and fail

## 🐛 If Something Seems Wrong

**Q: Hints aren't showing?**

- A: Make sure you're LOOKING AT the trash can (hover over it)

**Q: Can't find a specific trash can?**

- A: Press 1-5 to visit them in order, or Press T to find the nearest one

**Q: Teleported but still far from bin?**

- A: You should be very close! Click to dispose (you may need to aim carefully)

**Q: Forgot the controls?**

- A: Press H to open Help menu anytime!
